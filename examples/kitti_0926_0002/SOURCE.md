# KITTI raw: 2011_09_26_drive_0002

A car drives along a city street with tram tracks, trees and a brick wall. All 77 frames of the left color camera (`image_02`) of the synced and rectified sequence
`2011_09_26_drive_0002_sync`, 1242 x 375, 10 frames per second, re-encoded as JPEG (quality 95). The video depth benchmark of the paper
includes this sequence.

- Source: KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite, raw data, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago

  Research (IJRR), 2013.
