# TUM RGB-D: freiburg3_walking_xyz

Two people walk through an office while the camera moves along three directions with a fixed orientation. 90 frames of the sequence `rgbd_dataset_freiburg3_walking_xyz`
(every third color image from the third one, images 2 to 269 of the sequence, 10 frames per second; the 90 frames of the TUM-dynamics camera
benchmark of the paper),
640 x 480, re-encoded as JPEG (quality 95). The file names are the time stamps of the dataset.

- Source: TUM RGB-D dataset, Computer Vision Group, Technical University of Munich
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0); the text of the license is in `LICENSE.txt`
- Reference: J. Sturm, N. Engelhard, F. Endres, W. Burgard and D. Cremers. A Benchmark for the Evaluation of RGB-D SLAM Systems. IROS 2012.
